# Churn Distribution

Most customers remained retained, but a significant percentage churned, indicating opportunities for retention-focused strategies.


# Contract Type vs Churn

Customers with shorter contract commitments showed significantly higher churn rates, suggesting that long-term contracts improve customer retention.


# Average Monthly Charges

Customers with higher monthly charges tend to churn more frequently.
Pricing sensitivity may influence customer retention, especially among premium-paying users.


# Tenure vs Churn

Customer retention strategies should focus heavily on onboarding and early engagement periods.
